# Public and interview communication

Turbo is currently private, but its explanations are designed for future public
consumption and interview review.

## Three levels, one truth

1. Executive: purpose, strategy, architecture, result, limitations.
2. Analyst: factors, evidence, countercases, sensitivity, lineage.
3. Machine: immutable records, schemas, hashes, versions, receipts.

The first two are renderings of the third. They may simplify language but may not
change definitions, omit material uncertainty, or invent a cleaner result.

## Publication standard

- Expand acronyms on first use.
- State whether a row is a source category, canonical market, or submarket.
- Put confidence and evidence class beside scores.
- Call assumptions assumptions.
- Link quantitative claims to a source or run receipt.
- Distinguish current capability, planned design, and hypothesis.
- Do not publish personal, confidential, licensed, or restricted evidence.
- Make limitations and countercases easy to find.
- Keep README sufficient for an interviewer without chat history or a walkthrough.
