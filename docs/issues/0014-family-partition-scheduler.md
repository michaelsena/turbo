## Decision

Use one administrative family per six-digit NAICS leaf. Shared NAPCS collection
products retain every official appearance but receive one stable rendezvous-hash
owner. Unlinked collection identities enter bounded coverage-challenge families.
Aggregate NAICS and trilateral NAPCS identities are context-only. Prior candidates
have a separate collision ledger and one challenge owner. The 561110 pilot is pinned
as a completed regression fixture.

Administrative ownership is not a semantic market mapping, economic primacy,
eligibility decision, score, or company conclusion.
