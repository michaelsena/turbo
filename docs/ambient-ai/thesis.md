# Ambient AI thesis

Ambient AI continuously captures relevant operating context, connects it to enduring
entities and commitments, recognizes what changed, acts within authority, and verifies
completion. It differs from a user explicitly prompting a chatbot or automating one
isolated document.

## Workflow modes

Every market should estimate the share and importance of:

- structured desk work
- unstructured desk work
- routine field work
- unstructured field work
- coordination and exception handling across those modes

These are workflow descriptors, not a presumption that desk work is always more
automatable or that field work is unattractive.

## Market evaluation questions

1. What customer outcome is the business paid to complete?
2. What workflow currently bottlenecks accepted output?
3. Which context sources change what should happen next?
4. Can those sources be accessed lawfully, reliably, and economically?
5. What persistent entity or commitment connects the context over time?
6. What actions can the system take, and where is approval required?
7. How is completion verified and error detected?
8. Which physical, judgment, regulatory, or external constraints remain?
9. Can released capacity become revenue, or will demand/pricing absorb the gain?
10. What incremental earnings remain after implementation, review, and operating cost?
11. Is the capability distinctive versus ordinary software and standard AI tools?
12. Which context models, integrations, and workflows repeat across acquisitions?
13. Could AI eliminate the paid service itself rather than improve its delivery?

## Concrete concept requirement

A market cannot earn a high Ambient AI score from vague claims such as “automate
documents” or “improve workflows.” It needs a bounded first implementation with
named inputs, persistent entities, triggers, authorized actions, verification,
failure modes, human controls, measurable output, and retained economics.

## Cross-market archetype discovery

Turbo will cluster recurring mechanisms such as obligation monitoring, multi-party
coordination, exception resolution, evidence assembly, field-to-desk reconciliation,
schedule and dispatch adaptation, compliance state management, and quote-to-completion
orchestration. These archetypes may become the reusable platform core.
