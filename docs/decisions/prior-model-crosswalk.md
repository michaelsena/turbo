# Prior-model crosswalk

The earlier “Ambient AI: Ownership Thesis and Market Evaluation” is a design input
for Turbo. It is not imported as validated evidence.

## Carry forward

- Ownership economics must work without AI gains.
- Capture → Connect → Recognize → Act → Verify is the clearest Ambient AI loop.
- Recurring customer obligations are a useful cross-market hypothesis.
- Score every market; document estimates rather than leaving blanks.
- Durable demand, earnings quality, pricing, target availability, competitive
  opening, displacement resistance, completed-work gains, context dependence,
  feasibility, distinctiveness, retained value, and reuse remain valuable criteria.
- Pair quantitative rankings with deep operating cases and sensitivity tests.

## Improve in Turbo

- Replace a two-axis/50-50 score with six submodels and explicit hard gates.
- Replace a flat market table with a lineage-preserving, versioned Market Graph.
- Separate raw score, confidence, evidence quality, downside, and risk adjustment.
- Use independent specialists and mandatory adjudication rather than one scoring pass.
- Keep weak estimates active but continuously retry evidence gaps.
- Add pairwise close- and far-neighbor comparisons to calibrate global ranks.
- Separate global ranking, research queue, challenger intake, and shortlist.
- Make all source snapshots, score changes, and decisions replayable as of a cutoff.
- Expose results through an API and web application.

## Do not carry forward as conclusions

- The earlier list of 978 markets is not presumed complete or normalized correctly.
- Its scores and early leaders are hypotheses, not imported ground truth.
- The emerging recurring-obligations focus is a useful challenger lens, not a gate
  that narrows the new universe before independent evaluation.

This crosswalk will be expanded after the linked universe and evidence ledgers receive
an independent audit.
