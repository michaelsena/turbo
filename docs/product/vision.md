# Product vision

Turbo is the decision infrastructure for selecting markets in which to acquire and
scale American lower-middle-market service businesses with an Ambient AI operating
advantage.

Its customer is initially the chairman/investor. Its durable output is a versioned
market graph and ranked Target Market Universe whose conclusions can be traced to
evidence, inference, score policy, and exact run.

## Product principles

1. Universe quality precedes ranking quality.
2. Base-business attractiveness is independent of AI upside.
3. Ambient AI must map to a concrete bottleneck and completed-work outcome.
4. No blank scores: uncertainty changes confidence, not coverage.
5. Every score is inspectable and replayable.
6. The system continuously improves rather than periodically restarting.
7. Global rank, research priority, challenger intake, and shortlist are distinct.
8. The repository—not chat history—is the source of truth.

## V1 definition of done

- Preserve and validate one authoritative American industry hierarchy.
- Generate a provisional candidate set and explicit exclusions.
- Score every six-digit source category on one initial factor group.
- Produce raw score, confidence, risk-adjusted score, evidence reference, rationale,
  and active gap for every factor.
- Produce deterministic machine-readable outputs and local tests.
- Document what the first result does and does not mean.

V1 proves the loop; it does not claim that source taxonomies are finished markets.
