# Supplemental lease eligibility research

These seven primary-source research notes improve the evidence behind the held
lease-administration candidate. They do not constitute a provider-accepted gate
judgment, a frozen raw-source evidence pack, or authorization for another call.

The notes preserve retrieval dates, URLs, short verbatim excerpts, separately
labeled researcher paraphrases and limitations. Original HTML was not retained.
The manifest hashes the notes themselves; it must not be described as hashing
the original pages. Before a future governed run, capture the required source
snapshots and prepare new evidence bindings.

The strongest increment is Cresa's 2019 acquisition of Portfolio Solutions Group,
which establishes a US lease-services acquisition precedent and reported scale.
Neither transaction price nor EBITDA is disclosed. Software acquisitions and an
unrelated emergency-services agency's union agreement were excluded as mismatched
evidence. Agency-style payment controls are an inference from marketing material,
not proof of contractual allocation of credit or insurance risk.

All five unresolved gate questions remain open: target acquisition population,
buyability, government-dependent revenue, insurance/credit exposure and unionized
workforces. No paid eligibility rerun was made solely on this supplement.
