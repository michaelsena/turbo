# Prospective service-market eligibility research

These reviewed **research inputs remain unbound** to canonical market versions.
They are neither eligibility decisions nor investment scores. The two prospective
subjects are commercial occupier lease administration and outsourced community
association management. A future binding must replay the published canonical graph,
select exact market versions, retain the original strategy, and preserve every gap.
The governed eligibility runtime decides whether evidence supports a pass, hold, or
exclusion; it cannot infer a pass from this directory's existence.

## Research question and evidence boundaries

What public market-level evidence supports or contradicts the original strategy's
eligibility gates for these service markets? Evidence of a United States private-buyer
service population and relevant independent scale can support labeled feasibility
judgments. Union, government/reimbursement, principal credit/insurance, inventory,
or owned-equipment exposure can change the result toward hold or exclusion. Missing
evidence remains unknown. Revenue does not determine enterprise value; broad
industry/occupation proxies are not company diligence.

No company valuation, acquisition targeting, outreach, private financials, canonical
market creation, eligibility acceptance, or score creation is performed here.

| Artifact | Contents and limits |

| `evidence-draft.json` | Six source representations, seven labeled claims, five retained gaps, prospective subject/source mappings, and capture hashes; no canonical IDs |

The `raw_receipts.path` values identify original capture artifacts. The workbook is
intentionally not duplicated in this text-only repository increment. Retrieve its
public URL and verify the declared hash, or use the private audit. The selected-row
JSON is included here. Source and draft hashes remain exactly those independently
reviewed before staging.

## Unresolved gates

The research supplies some service-existence, workforce, population, and proxy
observations. It does **not** establish market-specific union exposure, independent
EV $10–50M target counts, buyability under the hard 9x ceiling, or all excluded
exposures. No union exemption or other eligibility exception is adopted. Non-US
provider evidence is labeled as an analogue and cannot establish United States
market-wide conditions.

Once exact canonical versions exist, a reviewed operator can bind the prospective
labels to those versions and prepare the existing eligibility work unit. Binding
must refuse absent or ambiguous versions; it must not manufacture a canonical ID,
substitute a classification category for a market, or rewrite a failed boundary
result. Evaluation remains downstream of successful governed eligibility.
