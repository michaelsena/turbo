Researcher paraphrase of FCAR 2024 national estimates, page 2: 9,000-10,000 community association management companies; 60,000-65,000 managers including onsite and part-time portfolio managers; 105,000-110,000 people employed by management companies and onsite associations with staff. 30-40% of associations self-manage. Homeowner assessments finance management, utilities, security, insurance, maintenance and capital work; assessment volume is not management-company revenue. These estimates establish neither independent ownership nor providers in an EV band, and include employment outside outsourced management companies.
