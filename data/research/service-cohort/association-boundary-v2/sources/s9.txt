VERBATIM EXCERPT:
A community association manager license is not applicable to the management of apartment buildings, commercial property or single-family dwellings.

RESEARCHER PARAPHRASE (not a quotation):
Florida DBPR describes compensated association management as administration of association funds, budgets and meetings, coordination of residential-development maintenance and day-to-day association services. Its FAQ distinguishes the CAM license from management of apartments, commercial property and single-family dwellings. It identifies the board as responsible for association operations and permits uncompensated self-management without hiring an outside manager. This is a Florida regulatory distinction; it is not a nationwide legal rule or proof that every operator specializes exclusively.
