Community association management companies.

RESEARCHER PARAPHRASE (not a quotation):
FCAR2024 national data estimate9,000–10,000 community association management companies and60,000–65,000 managers, including on-site and part-time portfolio support. It estimates30–40% of associations self-manage rather than employ a professional manager/company. Association assessments fund management plus utilities, insurance, maintenance, security and other obligations; they are not management-company revenue. Counts include varied association types and staffing arrangements, not a verified population of independent purchasable firms.
